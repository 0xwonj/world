# Content Systems

This document tracks candidate content systems that can produce combinatorial
variety without relying only on hand-authored special cases.

## Entity Content

- Species, ancestry, culture, profession, background.
- Body plan: limbs, organs, senses, equipment slots, vulnerabilities.
- Stats and skills.
- Traits, mutations, curses, blessings, implants, scars.
- Needs: hunger, thirst, sleep, safety, loyalty, faith, curiosity.
- Goals: survive, guard, trade, explore, avenge, worship, flee, recruit.

## Item Content

- Materials: wood, iron, glass, bone, cloth, crystal, flesh, fungus.
- Tags: flammable, sharp, conductive, brittle, edible, poisonous, sacred.
- Form: weapon, tool, container, book, key, relic, ingredient.
- State: temperature, durability, contamination, charge, ownership.
- Embedded knowledge: maps, names, rituals, passwords, recipes, histories.

## Environment Content

- Terrain: stone, soil, water, lava, chasm, fungus, ice, machinery.
- Fluids and gases.
- Temperature and pressure.
- Light, sound, smell, magical residue.
- Weather and seasonal conditions.
- Destructible or transformable walls and objects.

## Social Content

- Factions and reputation.
- Local laws and taboos.
- Social roles: guard, priest, merchant, outlaw, elder, exile.
- Promises, debts, rumors, secrets, crimes.
- Trade networks and scarcity.

## Knowledge Content

- Facts: verified world information.
- Beliefs: actor-specific and possibly false information.
- Rumors: uncertain information from social sources.
- Memories: time-stamped observations.
- Lore: historical or cultural knowledge.
- Secrets: restricted knowledge with gameplay value.

## Rule Families

- Fire, smoke, heat, and flammability.
- Acid, corrosion, and material damage.
- Electricity, conductivity, and wetness.
- Poison, disease, food, and contamination.
- Sound propagation and stealth.
- Light, darkness, vision, and concealment.
- Necromancy, corpses, spirits, and forbidden rituals.
- Law, reputation, witnessing, and crime.
